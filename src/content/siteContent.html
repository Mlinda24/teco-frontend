Who we are

TECO Farms & Engineering Limited

A Malawian company established in November 2021, with a commitment to delivering practical, reliable and sustainable solutions in water, engineering, agriculture and environmental management.

TECO Farms & Engineering Ltd brings together multidisciplinary technical expertise in Construction, surface water and groundwater, water engineering, irrigation, civil engineering and environmental assessment. Our approach combines field investigations, engineering analysis, design, construction supervision and technical consultancy to develop solutions that respond to site conditions and client requirements.

How we work

A clear process from first conversation to handover.

Every project is different. Our approach starts with understanding the brief and ends with practical documentation and a focus on sustainable operation.

STEP 01 / DISCOVER

Understand the need

Discuss project objectives, site context, community requirements, budget and expected outputs.

STEP 02 / ASSESS

Survey & investigate

Review available information and undertake the required site assessment, measurements and technical investigations.

STEP 03 / DESIGN

Plan the solution

Develop practical designs, drawings, specifications, schedules and cost information appropriate to the brief.

STEP 04 / DELIVER

Support & hand over

Coordinate implementation, supervise quality where commissioned, and provide handover documentation.

Who we are

Local understanding. Professional engineering.

TECO Farms & Engineering is a Malawi-based engineering and consultancy company providing services across water resources, water infrastructure, agriculture and civil works. We combine technical planning with practical delivery to help clients turn project needs into reliable outcomes.

We aim to build lasting working relationships with clients, communities and government stakeholders, with attention to quality, time management, safety and sustainability throughout each assignment.

  • Water supply and reticulation design
  • Borehole and pumping-test services
  • Engineering drawings and construction

How we work

A clear process from first conversation to handover.

Every project is different. Our approach starts with understanding the brief and ends with practical documentation and a focus on sustainable operation.

STEP 01 / DISCOVER

Understand the need

Discuss project objectives, site context, community requirements, budget and expected outputs.

STEP 02 / ASSESS

Survey & investigate

Review available information and undertake the required site assessment, measurements and technical investigations.

STEP 03 / DESIGN

Plan the solution

Develop practical designs, drawings, specifications, schedules and cost information appropriate to the brief.

STEP 04 / DELIVER

Support & hand over

Coordinate implementation, supervise quality where commissioned, and provide handover documentation.
